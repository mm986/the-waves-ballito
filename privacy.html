Privacy Policy

This website is a brochure site for The Waves Beachfront Stay, a self-catering apartment in Salt Rock, Ballito. It exists to show you the property and point you to Booking.com and Airbnb to check availability and book.

What this site collects

Nothing. This site has no sign-up forms, no accounts, and no payment processing. We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and no personal data is collected or stored by this website.

Booking platforms

When you click through to Booking.com or Airbnb to check rates or book, you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply to any information you provide them:

  • Booking.com Privacy Policy
  • Airbnb Privacy Policy

Embedded map

The location section on the homepage embeds a Google Map. Google may set its own cookies when that map loads — see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the host directly via a message through Booking.com or Airbnb once you've made an enquiry or booking.
