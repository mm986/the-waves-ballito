Welcome to The Waves

Your beachfront sanctuary in Salt Rock

In front of golden sands on the KwaZulu-Natal Dolphin Coast, The Waves is a recently renovated, modern 3-bedroom apartment built for families, friends and couples seeking a coastal escape. Relax on the private balcony with sunrise coffee, take a dip in the outdoor pool with sea and mountain views, or cook up a feast in the fully equipped kitchen.

Salt Rock Beach — lifeguard-patrolled and shark-net protected — is a two-minute stroll away, and everything from local cafés to the Ballito Farmers Market is within easy reach.

Salt Rock, Ballito

Right where you want to be

On the Dolphin Coast, 40 minutes north of Durban and 20–25 minutes from King Shaka International Airport.

  • Salt Rock Beach 2 min walk

    Lifeguard-patrolled and shark-net protected, with a tidal pool that's safe for kids and snorkelling.

  • Local Cafés Walking distance

    The Waterberry Café, Sage Café & Deli, Salt Café and Salt Rock Coffee Company are all close by.

  • Ballito Junction Mall 10 min drive

    Shopping, groceries and restaurants, plus the Ballito Farmers Market every Sat, Sun & public holiday, 8am–3pm.

  • King Shaka International Airport 20–25 min

    18 km away — an easy drive at the start or end of your trip.

  • Sugar Rush Park & Durban Day trips

    Bike trails, ziplining and a petting zoo at Sugar Rush Park; Durban is around 40 minutes south.
